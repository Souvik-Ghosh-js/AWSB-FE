## What was downloaded and deleted, and why

Of 14 images fetched, **11 were discarded after inspecting each one**:

- **Visible third-party trademarks** — Chanel N°5, Coco Noir, Coco Mademoiselle,
  Bleu de Chanel, Versace Eros, Prada Milano, Lancôme Poème, Dior Dune. The
  Unsplash licence covers the *photographer's* copyright; it grants nothing over
  a trademark inside the frame. Publishing these would imply an association that
  does not exist, and would misrepresent what a customer receives.
- **Off-subject** — a leather handbag, a pink birthday gift box, and a person in
  a neon horror mask.

Searching stock libraries for "perfume bottle" overwhelmingly returns designer
product photography, because that is what people photograph. Assume any new
stock image is branded until you have looked at it.

## Replacing these

Shoot the real bottles: a phone camera, a windowsill, and a plain sheet of paper
as backdrop is genuinely enough for fragrance. Upload through the admin panel —
product images are stored on the server and served from
`https://api.attarworldsonarbangla.com/uploads/`.

Files here are only for site furniture (hero, scent-family tiles, the about
page). Product photographs belong in the admin panel, not in this folder.
